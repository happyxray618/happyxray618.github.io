Privacy policy / 隱私政策

Updated: 30 October 2026

Pre-release status: Naptwo's backend has been deployed to a dedicated internal-test environment. It is not currently open to players; real-device business and app-attestation checks remain in progress. Local development verification does not constitute production security or payment validation. This page describes the implemented design and current limitations; it will be updated before an online test opens.

Data and purpose

The email-account implementation uses Firebase Authentication for email/password sign-in and password reset. Account IDs and sessions identify the signed-in account. Pet names, room choices, care events, invitation/application labels, predefined letters, read states, archives and block records support the shared-pet experience. We do not implement contacts/location collection, free-text chat, AI conversation, advertising or analytics.

Firebase/Google services process technical information for authentication, app attestation, operation and abuse prevention, including IP addresses, SDK/user-agent and app information, account identifiers, applicable messaging/installation identifiers, and Play Integrity tokens. A technical messaging token does not mean Naptwo has enabled player push notifications. The current cloud project has 30-day default operational logs and a locked 400-day required audit-log bucket. Scheduled Firestore backups and point-in-time recovery are disabled; underlying database version retention is one hour. These settings are not a promise of immediate erasure from every provider system; Google may retain security or legal records under its applicable policies. We do not sell personal data or implement advertising targeting.

Access and sharing

Current nest members share pet state. Letters retain their recipient boundary. Client access is scoped to the signed-in account and authorized nests. The backend can process these records; this is not end-to-end encryption between members. Online transport must use HTTPS; local development uses explicitly isolated loopback emulators.

Account and data deletion

The implemented in-app Delete account action requires password confirmation. It deletes the requesting account and shared nests, pets and history for both members. The other member's independent account, unrelated data and independent purchases remain. No real purchase is currently enabled. Re-registering a deleted email produces a new account, without the old pet/history.

Deletion is resumable across service failures. Only minimal task/recovery state is retained to complete it; completed task state is scheduled for removal after 24 hours, on the next hourly cleanup (normally 24–25 hours with a healthy worker). The hourly cloud worker is deployed and its empty-queue invocation has passed; complete real-cloud deletion recovery remains a verification gate before player access. The task-retention period is not a promise of unlimited offline recovery or a production SLA. See the deletion request page.

Payments

Real Google Play purchases, refunds and purchase restoration are not available. Developer-only simulated transactions are not real payments and are disabled for cloud accounts.

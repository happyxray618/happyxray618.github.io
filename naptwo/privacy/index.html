Privacy policy / 隱私政策

Updated: 5 November 2026

Naptwo version 10 is available on Google Play. V8 advertising changes (version code 11) are being prepared for internal testing; this does not mean advertising is enabled in the currently public version. Local tests, internal tests and public release have separate verification records.

Data and purpose

The email-account implementation uses Firebase Authentication for email/password sign-in and password reset. Account IDs and sessions identify the signed-in account. Pet names, room choices, care events, invitation/application labels, predefined letters, read states, archives and block records support the shared-pet experience. We do not request contacts or precise-location permissions, and do not offer free-text chat or AI conversation. Version 10 has no advertising. Advertising-enabled later builds use Google Mobile Ads and UMP as described below; SDK-derived approximate location is different from a location permission.

Firebase/Google services process technical information for authentication, app attestation, operation and abuse prevention, including IP addresses, SDK/user-agent and app information, account identifiers, applicable messaging/installation identifiers, and Play Integrity tokens. A technical messaging token does not mean Naptwo has enabled player push notifications. The current cloud project has 30-day default operational logs and a locked 400-day required audit-log bucket. Scheduled Firestore backups and point-in-time recovery are disabled; underlying database version retention is one hour. These settings are not a promise of immediate erasure from every provider system; Google may retain security or legal records under its applicable policies. We do not sell account or pet records. Advertising-enabled builds can use Google advertising processing under the applicable choices described below; we do not claim that ad SDKs collect no data.

Access and sharing

Current nest members share pet state. Letters retain their recipient boundary. Client access is scoped to the signed-in account and authorized nests. The backend can process these records; this is not end-to-end encryption between members. Online transport must use HTTPS; local development uses explicitly isolated loopback emulators.

Account and data deletion

The implemented in-app Delete account action requires password confirmation. It deletes the requesting account and shared nests, pets and history for both members. The other member's independent account, unrelated data and independent purchases remain. No real purchase is currently enabled. Re-registering a deleted email produces a new account, without the old pet/history.

Deletion is resumable across service failures. Only minimal task/recovery state is retained to complete it; completed task state is scheduled for removal after 24 hours, on the next hourly cleanup (normally 24–25 hours with a healthy worker). The hourly cloud worker is deployed. Dedicated-account deletion and recovery have bounded local/cloud test records; this is not a promise that every failure or device has been exhaustively tested. The task-retention period is not a promise of unlimited offline recovery or a production SLA. See the deletion request page.

Advertising and privacy choices

Advertising-enabled V8 builds integrate Google Mobile Ads and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). The public version 10 remains ad-free. The planned enabled placements are an adaptive home banner and an optional rewarded ad for a short, temporary pet-play animation. Neither changes pet vitality, happiness, growth, ownership or account entitlements. Native and interstitial consumer placements remain disabled until a suitable experience is designed and verified.

When ads are permitted, Google Mobile Ads may collect and share approximate location inferred from IP, device/account identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID, app interactions including ad views/taps, and diagnostic information. Purposes include advertising delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. We do not send Naptwo passwords, email addresses, invitation codes or private pet history as custom ad-targeting fields. Google may process information under its own service and privacy terms. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google advertising privacy.

UMP refreshes consent information before ads are requested and displays required regional choices. Where required, use the in-app Privacy choices entry to revisit them, including applicable European and US-state options. An advertising choice is not authorization for all other account processing. A failed or unavailable consent check leaves ad requests disabled; free core care remains usable. Deleting a Naptwo account removes our account-linked business records according to the deletion policy; Google advertising/security records have provider-specific legal and retention rules and are not promised immediate removal by our account-delete action.

Payments

Real Google Play purchases, refunds and purchase restoration are not available. Developer-only simulated transactions are not real payments and are disabled for cloud accounts.

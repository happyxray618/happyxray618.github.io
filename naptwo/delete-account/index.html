Account & data deletion
帳戶與資料刪除

This page is accessible without signing in or installing Naptwo.

Online service status: the backend is being prepared and is not open to players. The in-app deletion implementation has local test evidence; this does not mean cloud deletion has been deployed or verified.

Request deletion without the app

  1. Email service@gamebox.wiki from the email address associated with your Naptwo account. Use the subject “Naptwo account deletion request”.
  2. State that you want your Naptwo account and associated records deleted. Never include your password, tokens, invitation code, reset link or payment credentials.
  3. We must verify account ownership before acting. Sending a request is not proof that deletion has finished. You should receive a separate completion confirmation after processing; delivery and response time have not been independently verified.

Compose deletion request

This opens your email app. It does not send anything automatically. You must send the email yourself. No request or account information is collected by this static page.

Using the app

Sign in, choose Delete account, read the shared-data warning and confirm your password. If the result is uncertain, use the saved deletion recovery action; do not assume that a connection error means either success or cancellation.

What is deleted?

The requesting Auth identity/profile, associated invitations and applications, shared nests and pets (including old archives), care events, letters, replacement requests, block relationships, account entitlements and developer mock transaction records. Shared nest and history removal affects both members. The other person's independent account, unrelated records and independent entitlements remain.

Temporary task state and limits

A minimal task/recovery record is needed for failure recovery. Completed task cleanup is designed for 24–25 hours with a healthy hourly worker. Cloud deployment, backup/log retention and operational monitoring still need verification before player access. No real billing exists; future legally required transaction retention would need a revised disclosure before enabling payments.
